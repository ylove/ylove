## 🛠️ What I build

Right now, what I'm working on:

- **Vouch Networks**, top to bottom: React Native app, Next.js web, Node/Postgres backend, payments and compliance, and every founder job that doesn't fit on a slide.
- **Agentic systems that run unattended.** Multi-agent workflows in Claude Code with a layered intent doctrine so agents know *why* they're doing something, not just what. Local inference on hardware I built. Hierarchical multi-agent systems. I optimize for reliability, observability, and cost, not demo-day magic.
- **Marketplace and front-end leadership.** Most recently tech lead on a healthcare marketplace: owned the product surface, cleaned up analytics integrity, kept a cross-functional team shipping.
- **Legacy rescue.** I've untangled enough hand-rolled front-ends and SOAP-era integrations to know why the modern stack looks the way it does. That's an asset when your codebase has layers.

## 🛡️ Vouch Networks

<a href="https://vouch.media"><img align="right" src="https://img.shields.io/badge/vouch.media-visit-7c3aed" alt="vouch.media" /></a>

**Community safety, built by the communities that need it.** Vouch is a platform for incident reporting, verified community alerts, and resilient communications, starting with the Jewish community and expanding to other targeted groups. Also media analysis and bias detection for journalists and researchers.

What's public: invite-gated, provenance-first onboarding (every account has a traceable path), a mobile-first React Native app, and an off-grid communications layer in the works. Friends-and-family round closed, hundreds of pre-registrations ahead of launch.

**Looking to partner?** Community organizations, security professionals, and folks in public safety tech: let's talk. → [vouch.media](https://vouch.media) or [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/yitzjordan).
